Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

MasMedia Stories ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload his own videos, titles and descriptions to his own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts.

What data the app uses

The app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos and their metadata to the owner's channels. It stores the OAuth access tokens for those channels locally on the owner's computer. It does not access, collect, store or share data from any other person or YouTube user.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also complies with the YouTube Terms of Service, and users can review Google's Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access makes the app delete the stored tokens on next use.
